<br>
Here is **Mingyang Chen (陈铭阳)**.<br>
<br>
I am an M.S.S. student in Criminology and Criminal Justice at the University of Macau. Before studying at the University of Macau, I got an LL.B. degree from China University of Political Science and Law and also passed the China Bar Exam. My research interests include criminal justice decision-making, machine and human in the criminal justice system, and empirical legal studies. <br>
<br>
Recently, our work got the best paper honorable mention award at the ***AAAI2026 Bridge program***. Our recent work for analyzing judge decision behavior using NLP tools is published online in the journal ***Law, Probability & Risk***. <br>
<br>
