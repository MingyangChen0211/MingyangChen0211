**Author**: Mingyang Chen, Zhipeng Wu

Go to this [Link](https://github.com/MingyangChen0211/Chinese.legalText) to see the source code.

This package is for analyzing criminal sentencing documents. We wrote several functions to help a starter to quickly learn how to complete an empirical analysis on CJO data. Several researchers have been using CJO data, but most of them do not report how they get data from their documents. According to our interview, many scholars are still using research assistants to read documents or read on their own, or ask for help from certain companies or computer scientists. These methods are expensive, time-consuming, and, to some extent, prevent scholars to conduct futher complex research.

To help legal and criminological scholars using CJO data, we develop this package. We provide several baseline methods for users, and you are free to use all functions here. This package is not limited to analyzing Chinese criminal texts. You can use this package to calculate the similarity of your own text data. A potential application is to calculate the similarity of patent documents, or extract legal factors in civil law documents. Please note that this package can not help you do everything, but can really save your time and reduce your study costs. Also, it can be used to replicate other studies.

We hope legal and criminological researchers from China, Taiwan, Macau, Hong Kong, and other regions using Chinese or familiar with Chinese to conduct replication studies for existing CJO research using this open-source package. We hope to build an OPEN-SOURCE Chinese legal and criminological research community!
